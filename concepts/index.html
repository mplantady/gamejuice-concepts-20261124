Each pass uses one identical prompt across all 12 games: a shared layout-preservation instruction plus the selected treatment. Every image starts from its original screenshot; the treatments are not applied in sequence. Open a comparison for review notes on changes to layout, copy, or artwork beyond the requested treatment.

Pass 2 is retained as an archived exploration of flat cel colors and hand-drawn character treatments. Its images and videos are concept studies; review notes record style and gameplay limitations.

Pass 3 preserves each game’s original artwork, materials, shading and lighting. The main quality push is game feel: expressive motion, event-timed effects and layered sound. Compare the Concept Video with the original and explore earlier rounds below.
