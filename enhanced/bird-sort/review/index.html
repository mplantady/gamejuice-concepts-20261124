What matches — and what does not

MatchesGaps to close

Board and gameplay. 14 branches, 50 birds, 10 species and the original matching/capacity rules. The three transfers finish with L1: 4, L4: 5 mixed, R3: 1 and R4: 2; no branch clears incorrectly.

Timing and guide hand. Every playable flight is 1.26s, shorter than the video. Demo hints last 430–440ms, but the hand starts travelling after 1,300ms, so it disappears before reaching the destination.

Art direction. Dusk forest, crescent moon, curved reddish branches, cartoon outlines and cel shading remain. The three demo destination positions match the video.

Artwork. Some feet/toes and thin outlines are clipped, especially on gray birds and toucans. One reused pose per species, regularized perch positions and a rebuilt background also change the picture.

Motion vocabulary. Anticipation, curved flights, wing flaps, direction changes, landing squash and short accents are present.

Motion details. Wingbeats are 5.41Hz versus 3.50Hz in the video, about 55% faster. Arcs differ; the red pair is staggered by 80ms instead of moving together, and its individual routes are swapped even though the final slots are correct.

Narration. The playable uses the same voice recording and mechanics explanation.

Audio mix. Nominal narration starts at 0.00s instead of 0.35s. Effects are rebuilt, and the video’s quiet music bed is missing. It is not the same mix.

First artwork repair: restore feet and thin contours. The missing toes are in the extracted sprite masks, not just hidden by branches or reduced screenshot size.

Next fixes, in order

  1. Align timing and the hand

    Use the video’s flight and landing times, slow wingbeats to 3.50Hz, and let the guide hand reach each destination before committing the move.

  2. Repair details, then match paths

    Restore clipped feet and thin outlines first. Match source positions and arcs, launch the red pair together, and add source-faithful pose variants for repeated birds.

  3. Match the audio timeline

    Offset narration by 0.35s, restore the quiet music bed, and match the flutter, landing accents and their levels to the video.
