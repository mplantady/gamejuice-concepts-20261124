What version 2 fixes

Matched to the Concept VideoWhat to know

Choreography and hand. The same six taps lead into the same three flights. Arcs, direction-change timing, anticipation, landing bounce and accents follow the video. The red pair flies together on the correct individual routes.

A complete 12-second demo. The hand reaches each source and destination. Visuals follow the soundtrack’s playback clock; ordinary browser scheduling can still affect individual displayed frames.

Native perched artwork. Feet and thin contours are restored. Individual source cutouts retain the original opening poses and positions, and the demo begins with the source still before entering separate animated layers.

Revealed artwork. When a bird moves, a complete bird pose replaces its stationary source cutout. Previously hidden background and body areas are reconstructed; those new pixels are not an exact source-image copy.

Flight cadence. All ten species use full-body, eight-pose animations. The interactive animation cycle follows the video’s approximately 3.50 wingbeats per second, without the old extra body rotation or faster wing overlay.

An intentional upgrade. Wings, body, tail and feet now animate together. The new flight anatomy and silhouettes are richer than the Concept Video’s simple wing treatment, so in-flight poses deliberately differ.

The same demo soundtrack. The playable uses a byte-identical copy of the video’s complete mix: narration, quiet music, flutters, taps, landings and ducking, including narration’s 0.35-second start.

Interactive play has separate cues. Outside the demo, actions trigger the matching local effects and music. They are suppressed during the demo so the premixed soundtrack is not doubled.
